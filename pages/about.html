The idea behind the platform

A more direct route from farm to home

Farm2Home is an academic prototype built around the value of knowing where our food comes from.

About Farm2Home

Small farms deserve a place in the digital marketplace.

Farm2Home demonstrates how a simple web experience can connect customers with agricultural products and the people who grow them. It is designed as a BTech Full Stack Lab mini project, with all order and marketplace activity simulated in the browser.

Project objective: explore a clear farm-to-consumer shopping journey.
Farmer support concept: make growers, locations, and methods visible.
Customer experience: browse, save favorites, order, and follow a demo delivery.
Future improvements could include secure accounts, verified payments, and live logistics.
